New tokens
--penalty #8E0B24 — the strike moment. Darker and less saturated than the pink field, so it never reads as brand pink; used only inside cream cards (word mid-strike, UNIQUE numeral), never on the pink field.
--penalty-soft #BA1B40 — the feathered ring bleeding in from inside the card's edge on a strike. Only ever used feathered and only ever inside a card, so it never competes with the pink field; a flat fill at card scale would strobe when three cards react at once.
--silver #C9CDD4 — 2nd place plaque + card offset shadow.
--bronze #C08457 — 3rd place. Warm enough to sit beside cream, dark enough to hold ink text.
Decisions
Empty lists in Frame 1. Blank list area, no "Nothing written." — that string means the player wrote nothing, and using it here would lie for four seconds. Vertical budget goes to the lists. Card padding 14 → 10px, head block gap 4px, hairline margin 7px, and in team play the member avatars sit above RANK n at 15px rather than below the stats. Roughly 22px per card returned to the word list — a line and a half at 15px. Team badge sizes to its name. Shrink-to-fit up to the card width minus its 10px insets, then it clips and the name travels. That needs a measured travel distance rather than a container query — `container-type` zeroes the badge's intrinsic width, which is what was collapsing it — so every clipped run gets `--travel` (clip width − text width, clamped at 0) written after render. Anything that fits keeps its natural width and never moves. The avatar trail scrolls too. Four or five attributions overflow the 40% of the row the trail can spare, and a clipped emoji is a missing attribution. The clip box carries the pop, the inner run carries the travel — one transform each, so they never fight. Room for the name. A name longer than five characters was colliding with the stats, because name, RANK and both stat pairs shared one row inside a 220px track. The identity block is now two lines: avatar + name across the full card width, then RANK n and the stat pair beneath it. The name gets ~4× the room, nothing else moves, and the card grows by 6px. RANK in Frame 1. Reads "—". Entry order is not rank, hiding the line would shift the row's height, and a placeholder number would be read as a standing. Inter-column beat. Yes, one — 420ms default, a separate tweak from the line interval. Flat cadence within a column, one breath between them, so the room can see attention move sideways. Strike duration is independent. A strike owns its 300ms regardless of the interval: at 900ms there is no dead air (the strike finishes, the beat continues), and at 90ms strikes simply overlap on different rows rather than truncating. The strike reaction is a flinch, not a fill. A feathered penalty ring bleeding in from inside the card's edge, plus a weighted dip down with a small overshoot back — the card reacts physically instead of changing colour. It sits on an overlay child so the hard offset shadow is untouched, and the feathering is the one place blur is allowed: it is a light effect inside the card, never a shadow under it. Podium, flat-graphic. A tilted plaque on the house −2.5° angle over the card's top-right, plus the card's hard offset shadow recolouring ink → medal. No glow, no blur, no sparkle. Ties share a place. Equal unique and equal total ⇒ same RANK n, and the next place skips (1, 2, 2, 4). Two firsts means two gold plaques and no silver. Crossing paths. The wrapper travels in Y, the card in X with a 180ms delay — vertical first, then across — and the moving card lifts (z-index, 1.5° tilt, scale 1.03). Cards therefore never swap through each other at the same height. Reduced motion end state. Final order, real RANK n, all lists fully revealed, all strikes applied with final UNIQUE counts, podium plaques and medal shadows present, footer showing STANDINGS. No transform, no blink, no pop — everything at its end value on first paint.
Runtime arithmetic
Runtime = (total lines × line interval) + (scorers × inter-column pause). Nothing is batched or accelerated. 3 × 10 words — 30 lines × 260ms + 3 × 420ms = 9.1s 10 × 35 words — 350 lines × 260ms + 10 × 420ms = 95.2s Recommended default interval 260ms: fast enough that a 30-line round is under ten seconds, slow enough that a strike and its emoji trail land as one readable beat. 95s for a ten-player wall is acceptable — FAST FORWARD is the escape valve, not compression. Footer: FAST FORWARD → STANDINGS
Two identities, never two golds. During the sequence the footer holds FAST FORWARD as a cream secondary: cream fill, ink text, 3px ink border, 3px offset shadow, 14px Bungee — the app's "sideways" vocabulary. Gold with a 4px border and a 5px shadow stays reserved for STANDINGS, the forward action. Fast forward is a compressed run, not a hard cut. Press → every outstanding strike lands on the same frame, and the whole grid takes one 320ms penalty wash together as the acknowledgement, then Frame 3 runs at 1.6× speed. The room sees "that all happened" instead of a jump cut, and the podium still arrives as a moment. The double-press guard is three things at once. (1) Position: FAST FORWARD sits at the footer's left inset, STANDINGS is centred — they never share a hit target. (2) FAST FORWARD exits over 160ms and STANDINGS only begins entering after a 480ms hold, so the footer is deliberately empty in between. (3) STANDINGS carries pointer-events:none for its 280ms entry. Total dead window ≈ 920ms — longer than a mash interval, and the empty footer is what tells the host to stop pressing.
